CHETESAÍ FITNESS+ METHODOLOGYTrain smart · Improve with a method
A REALISTIC, PROGRESSIVE AND MEASURABLE APPROACH

Train smart.
Improve with a method.

The Chetesaí Fitness+ methodology starts with an initial assessment, turns your starting point into a personalised plan and sustains progress through support, measurement and adjustments. The aim is to avoid generic routines and build a process that fits each person's real life.

PERSONALISED

Every plan starts with your goals, level, availability and circumstances.

PROGRESSIVE

Training load and difficulty increase in a structured way.

MEASURABLE

Progress is observed, recorded and reviewed regularly.

SUSTAINABLE

The method prioritises adherence, technique and consistency.

How it works

01

Request your assessment

The process begins with a conversation about your goals, availability and preferred training format. This initial contact helps define what you need and how training should fit into your life.

02

We assess and plan

We analyse your starting point and create a realistic proposal. Assessment and personalised planning provide the basis for training structure, technique, frequency and monitoring.

03

You train and progress

Training is supported, measured and adjusted. The plan evolves with your response, progress and the needs that emerge along the way.

The five operational pillars

1:1Personal trainingSessions adapted to your goals, level and lifestyle.
2–4Small groupsTechnical attention and quality without losing individualisation.
↻Ongoing monitoringRegular assessments and adjustments to keep progressing.
▤Adapted routineA realistic, progressive plan designed specifically for each person.
✓Technique and progressImprove how you move and turn that improvement into measurable results.

Principles of the method

PERSONAL SUPPORT

A plan comes with ongoing support to correct, adjust and keep you on track.

PROFESSIONAL PLANNING

Work is structured with clear goals, an understanding of your starting point, appropriate frequency and training progression.

INDIVIDUAL ADAPTATION

The methodology integrates training, nutrition and habits within the professional services offered, adapting the process to each person's circumstances.

REAL PROGRESS

The goal is to build realistic, measurable and sustainable improvement, rather than pursue quick promises or generic routines.

Monitoring, health and safety

Chetesaí Fitness+ collects the information needed to personalise your plan: goals, injuries or limitations, routines, loads, attendance, nutrition, habits and progress. If we identify a risk requiring healthcare assessment, we may adapt or pause an activity.

Notice: content and plans are for physical conditioning and education. They do not replace diagnosis, treatment, physiotherapy or medical care.
